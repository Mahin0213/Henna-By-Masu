Bridal mehndi at home in Birmingham

Most Birmingham brides have their mehndi at home, two days before the wedding. Full bridal work — hands, feet and forearms — takes five to six hours, so the session is planned around your day rather than squeezed into it.

The consultation can be done by video, so there is no trip to Leicester before the day. Send photographs of the outfit and jewellery and the names or motifs you want worked in; a drawn proposal follows, refined once before the date.

Mehndi nights, parties and events

For mehndi nights, engagements and birthdays, the table is set up wherever the guests are — a front room, a hall or a hotel function suite. Designs are quick and complete, and the cost is agreed by the hours and the number of guests before the night.

Community events, school fairs and corporate days in Birmingham can be booked the same way, as a henna stall for the day.

Organic henna, never black henna

Every cone is mixed in the studio from henna powder, lemon, sugar and essential oils. Black henna, still sold at some stalls, contains PPD — a hair dye that burns skin — and is never used here.

Questions

Henna in Birmingham: your questions

Do you travel to Birmingham for bridal mehndi?

Yes, Birmingham is covered as standard. Bridal mehndi is drawn at your home or venue, ideally two days before the wedding.

How much does bridal mehndi cost in Birmingham?

Bridal mehndi starts from £50 and semi bridal from £30, with the consultation included. The final quote depends on the design and coverage, and travel is confirmed with it.

Can you do a henna table for a mehndi night in Birmingham?

Yes. Guest tables run at around ten to fifteen small designs an hour and are quoted by the hours and the number of guests.

How far ahead should Birmingham brides book?

Three months is comfortable. For a Saturday between May and October, or around Eid, three to six months is usual.

Do I need to come to Leicester for the consultation?

No. The consultation can be done by video, and the drawn proposal is sent to you before the day.
