How mehndi is booked

The bride

A full session of her own, held two days before the wedding so the stain reaches its darkest on the day.

The family

Mother, sisters and close family after the bride — fuller hands with dense panels and open ground.

The guests

Questions

How long does bridal mehndi take?

Five to six hours for full hands, feet and forearms. Semi bridal is around two. Guest tables run for as long as the evening needs.

When should the mehndi be held?

Two days before the wedding is ideal. One day works. The same morning rarely gives the stain time to deepen.

Can you do a whole family in one visit?

Yes, that is the usual booking. Give us numbers and we will plan the timings.
