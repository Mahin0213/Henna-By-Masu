Questions

How far ahead should I book?

Three months is comfortable. For a Saturday between May and October, or around Eid, three to six months is usual.

Can I bring my own design ideas?

Please do. Photographs, motifs, names, a piece of jewellery — anything that tells us what the design should carry.

Do you travel for weddings?

Yes. Leicester and the Midlands as standard; Luton, Northampton and London as full-day bookings with travel in the quote.
