Karva Chauth

Karva Chauth mehndi

Mehndi for the fast, drawn the day before so the colour is deep by moonrise.

Karva Chauth falls in October or early November, and the mehndi is usually drawn the day before the fast, so the stain has a full day to deepen by the evening puja. Designs run from a simple back-hand pattern to traditional full hands, with the moon, the sieve and the karwa worked in if you would like them.

The days before Karva Chauth are among the busiest of the year. Book early, especially for a group — sisters, friends, a whole family drawn in one visit.

Questions

When should I have my Karva Chauth mehndi?

The day before the fast is usual. It gives the stain a full day to darken to its deepest brown.

How far ahead should we book?

As early as you can. The days before Karva Chauth fill quickly, and group bookings need the most time.

Can you draw the moon and the karwa?

Yes. Traditional motifs — the moon, the sieve, the karwa, the couple — can be worked into the design. Tell us what you would like when you book.
